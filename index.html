01 / ABOUT

More than one perspective.

A foundation shaped by the study of people, institutions, and the systems that connect them.

My interests sit at the intersection of human behavior, criminal justice, research, and technology.

I hold a Bachelor of Arts in General Psychology and a Master of Arts in Criminal Justice, with a focus on Economic Crime Investigation.

I am continuing to develop my technical foundation through software engineering studies and practical projects. I am especially interested in how digital environments shape behavior and affect the way we understand security.

I approach technology as both a technical and a human subject: systems are built by people, used by people, and experienced within social contexts.
